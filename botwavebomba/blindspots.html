BLINDSPOTS

A blindspot is a story covered by one information bloc — and absent from another. Not balance. Not bias. Omission. The TELOS+PAI blindspot analyzer cross-references Western, adversarial, and non-aligned coverage of the same events to surface what one side isn't telling you.

— active blindspots detected · Updated every 6 hours by global_ingestor.py
WESTERN-ONLY BLINDSPOT

Story covered by BBC, NYT, CNN, Reuters — but absent or framed inversely by RT, IRNA, CGTN, Sputnik. The adversarial world is not covering this. Ask why.

ADVERSARIAL-ONLY BLINDSPOT

Story that RT, IRNA, and Sputnik are amplifying — while AP, BBC, and the Guardian are silent or burying it. These are the stories that will surprise you.
