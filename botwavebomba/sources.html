WHAT ARE THE 5 AXES?

INTERVENTIONIST
-1.0 = peace / +1.0 = pro-military

Does this outlet support or oppose military intervention? High scores = outlets that routinely frame US/NATO military action as necessary or legitimate.

ZIONIST
-1.0 = critical of Israel / +1.0 = pro-Israel

How does coverage frame Israeli state action? Scores derived from language patterns, sourcing, and framing of Gaza/West Bank coverage.

ATLANTICIST
-1.0 = multipolar / +1.0 = US-led world order

The primary bloc-classification axis. High = assumes US primacy is legitimate. Low = frames US hegemony as contested or illegitimate.

STATIST
-1.0 = anti-state / +1.0 = pro-state authority

Does the outlet challenge or reinforce state power? State media scores high. Libertarian/dissident outlets score low.

FINANCIALIZED
-1.0 = anti-market / +1.0 = pro-market finance

Does the outlet treat financial capitalism as natural or as a system to interrogate? High = Bloomberg/FT framing. Low = economic justice framing.
