WHY A TRADES SECTION?

The trades build everything. The press covers almost none of it. When a crane collapses, the local paper runs the obituary. When a union contract expires, the business section covers the stock impact — not the workers. When HUD changes a regulation, nobody outside the trades even knows.

BOTWAVEBOMBA applies the same 5-axis bias fingerprint to trades coverage that it does to geopolitics. Western outlets frame labor as a cost. Non-aligned outlets frame it as a right. Adversarial outlets frame it as western hypocrisy. The gap IS the story.
