Choose a country to build a local feed.

Filter the world feed to stories where at least one source is from your region. No location permission. No tracking. We don't know where you are; we filter on the country_code already attached to every source. Privacy is the differentiator.

Or auto-detect from browser (one-time, you can deny)

Click below to ask your browser for an approximate location, then we'll resolve the country code client-side via Mozilla's free reverse-geocode service. Nothing leaves your browser except the coordinate-to-country lookup (which doesn't include your IP). No cookies, no profile, no log.

Adversarial sources are included by design. If a story has a TRT, RT, or IRNA source from your region, it appears here. Western press blackout? You see what Tehran prints alongside what AP prints.
