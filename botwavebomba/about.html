THE METHOD

Every system that tells you what to read has a hidden architecture. Here is ours. Fully documented. Every component named. Every assumption declared.

What BOTWAVEBOMBA Is

BOTWAVEBOMBA is the public face of a private journalism research infrastructure called TELOS+PAI — a Purpose-Automated Intelligence substrate built to support serious investigative work. Every article you see here was ingested, scored, clustered, and framing-analyzed by an automated pipeline running against 244 global news sources, updated every six hours.

It is not a news aggregator in the Google News sense. It is not a fact-checker. It is a framing delta engine: the goal is to show you what language different information blocs use to describe the same event, and where the gaps — the blindspots — live.

Why Not Left / Center / Right?

Ground News, AllSides, and similar services organize sources on a left–center–right axis calibrated to American domestic politics. That axis is useful in a country where the main conflict is between two parties managing the same system. It is useless when you are trying to understand what the Iranian press says about the Strait of Hormuz, what the Swedish press says about NATO, or what the Chinese press says about Taiwan.

BOTWAVEBOMBA uses five independent axes, each scored from -1.0 to +1.0:

  • Atlanticist — does this outlet assume US-led world order is legitimate?
  • Interventionist — does this outlet support military intervention?
  • Zionist — how does this outlet frame Israeli state action?
  • Statist — does this outlet reinforce or challenge state authority?
  • Financialized — does this outlet treat financial capitalism as natural?

These axes produce a five-dimensional fingerprint per source — not a simple left/right label. A source can be anti-interventionist and pro-state simultaneously (much of the RT editorial line). A source can be pro-market and anti-zionist simultaneously (some European financial press on Israel/Palestine coverage). The fingerprint captures that complexity. A label cannot.

The TELOS+PAI Pipeline

The pipeline runs in seven stages:

  1. global_ingestor.py — pulls RSS feeds and article full-text from all 244 sources, deduplicates by content hash, stores to news_cache.jsonl.
  2. event_clusterer.py — groups articles about the same event using entity overlap and semantic proximity. A cluster = one story, many framings.
  3. bias_scorer.py — scores each article on the five-axis system using propaganda lexicon matching, agency-verb analysis, and source fingerprint weighting. Score range: -6 to +6 per article.
  4. framing_differ.py — within each cluster, compares headlines, ledes, verb choices, and named entities across sources. Produces the framing comparison table you see on each story page.
  5. blindspot_analyzer.py — cross-references Western vs adversarial vs non-aligned coverage volume per cluster. If one bloc covers a story and another doesn't, that gap is surfaced as a blindspot with a score.
  6. coverage_mapper.py — generates the heatmap distribution (Western / Neutral / Adversarial) per story and the bias variance score. High variance = the blocs disagree sharply on this story.
  7. broadcast.py — serializes the pipeline output to /api/latest.json and /api/blindspots.json, which this site reads. Updated every six hours by systemd timer.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not editorialize. The framing comparison shows the delta; you read it.
  • We do not write summaries. Every "summary" field is extracted from the original article.
  • We do not rate factuality of individual claims. We rate source-level factuality patterns using MBFC data.
  • We do not suppress adversarial sources. RT and Sputnik are in the index, labeled clearly, sourced directly. Suppression is the problem we are solving for.
  • We do not invent. Every claim traceable to a document, a URL, a byline.

The Journalism Connection

BOTWAVEBOMBA is the public surface of a private investigative research substrate that supports long-form journalism. The same 244-source pipeline that powers this site powers primary-source discovery for book-length investigation — the kind that requires knowing not just what AP reported, but what IRNA, Lenta.ru, and SCMP said on the same day about the same event, in their original framing, before any translation layer added editorial distance.

The methodology is the same as the journalism: primary sources or nothing. Every source URL is live. Every score is computed, not assigned. Every blindspot is measured against the cache, not asserted.

Bias Scoring Baseline

The five-axis scores are derived from three sources, hand-reconciled:

  • AllSides Media Bias Ratings — public ratings where available
  • MBFC (Media Bias/Fact Check) — factuality and bias ratings
  • TELOS+PAI hand-curation — for sources not rated by AllSides/MBFC, or where the existing rating does not capture the five-axis complexity. Methodology: editorial stance analysis of 50+ articles per source, entity framing patterns, named-state-alignment patterns, institutional-alignment signals.

Scores were generated on 2026-04-23 for the initial 208-source set and extended to 244 sources. The registry is versioned. Updates are logged.
