WHY TRACK THE MONEY?

Campaign finance filings are public record. CA Secretary of State Form 460 captures every donation over $100; FEC Schedule A/B is the federal mirror. The money tells you who benefits when a politician votes, signs a contract, or changes a regulation. This tracker ingests filings automatically, flags large donations from entities with state business, and surfaces them here with filed-document receipts.

BE UNDENIABLE. Every claim is backed by a named, filed, publicly-verifiable primary source. Every row links to its FEC or SOS receipt. The Discord drop and this page are the same substrate.
