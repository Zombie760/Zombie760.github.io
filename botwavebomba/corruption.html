WHY TRACK THE MONEY?

Campaign finance filings are public record. CA Secretary of State Form 460 captures every donation over $100. The money tells you who benefits when a politician votes, signs a contract, or changes a regulation.

This tracker ingests filings automatically, flags large donations from entities with state business, and generates tweet-ready exposes with filed document receipts. No opinion. Just the trail.
