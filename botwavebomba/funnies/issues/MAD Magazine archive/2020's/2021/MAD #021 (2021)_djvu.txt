In MAD's basebrawl, one point is scored for each base 
attained, plus a two point bonus for a home run, or five 
points in all. A “fear goal” is worth two points and is 
achieved by kicking the ball into the opposition’s dugout 
right after a home run. The other team then gets to bat.
