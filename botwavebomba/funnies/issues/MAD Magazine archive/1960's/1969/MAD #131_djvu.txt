bad days, when my heart leaped into my throat. High 
in the air over the most impenetrable part of the Park, 
slightly south of 99th Street, I spied a covey of En- 
glish Sparrows! 

“English Sparrows!!” 1 shouted at the top of my 
voice through trembling lips,
