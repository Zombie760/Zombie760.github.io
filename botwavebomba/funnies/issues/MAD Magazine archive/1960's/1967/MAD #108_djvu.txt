GROWTH RECORD 
At 19, I am entering my tenth year as 

a MAD reader. In that interval, 1 have 
watched, often amazed, always amused, 
che gradual transition of MAD from a 
rather crude, boorish comic book into the 
polished satiric masterpiece it is today. 
It seems that MAD grew up as I did, and 
complimented my maturing tastes. It 
helped me shape my attitudes and opin- 
ions, and taught me never to regard them 
as the only possible ones, It made me see 
the world in a new and different light, 
pointing our and exposing the absurd 
and ridiculous which surrounds us, and 
which we often take too seriously. To you, 
I say, thanks, 

Francis J. Mills 

Coplay, Pennsylvania
