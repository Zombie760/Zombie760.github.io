І grew ир іп a conservative home ruled by old-time religion. This is а treacherous path for апу juvenile to tread, especially іп the “805, when so much materialism was 
unvirtuous. MAD Magazine was no exception. MAD was decadent eye candy for prepubescent boys like me who were drawn to artistic media—and it was forbidden 
due to its ability to corrupt young minds. So І had to sneak down to the liquor store after school, and for the “cheap” price of 51.35 (which was often paid with loose 
change) | could go home with a brand-new, slightly fingered issue of MAD. | would hide this contraband іп my camouflage backpack, between my math and phonics

of caricature is as much in the hands as it is in the face. And Spy vs. Spy taught me to trust no one. Now that I’m an adult, I look back and agree 100 percent with my
