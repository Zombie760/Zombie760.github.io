We've totally rewritten it to make it appear as benign and transparent as possible. If you'd like more information, you can click here.

when necessary. 


What's different about the new Google Terms of Service? 
For starters, we're actively promoting it to make it seem like we're not a monolithic corporate behemoth. And how are we doing that? By

What's staying the same with Google's approach to privacy? 
We want you to know that we at Google remain absolutely committed to maintaining privacy — ours! Why do you think we don't bother posting 
our phone number and make it so difficult for you to contact us?

It's easy to change your settings to indicate you'd like more control over your personal information. Go to Google.com and in the upper right-

Is Google collecting more information about me? 
Absolutely not! Our previous policy was quite proficient in collecting everything possible there is to know about you, including your credit history,

itin a moment's notice and bombard you with dozens of ads every time you look at your computer screen. We've also created a fully searchable

Nothing at first. The whole point is that you won't even notice the changes. Then we can quietly collect all your information and slowly take over
