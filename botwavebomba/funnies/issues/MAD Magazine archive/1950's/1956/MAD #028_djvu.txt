Carelessness somewhere along the stamp pro- 
duction line often creates extremely valuable 
items, These are usually rare because as soon 
as the posta] authorities discover their errors 
they correct chem. Common examples are: 
A) Imprint on paper, forgetting perforations.

. €) Perforations and imprint, forgetting paper.
