The Lady was Surprised 


Mrs. Tillie Eichorn, whose first Pest 
Fiction, “HONEYMOON IN TEHERAN,” 
appears on page 26, drops us a card from 
her home near Conway, Ark., to say that 
she didn't start out to be a writer at all. 

"Great balls of fire!" messages Mrs. 
Eichorn, “All I did was write a letter to 
Montgomery Ward complaining that the 
milking stool they sent me wobbles! 
Imagine my surprise when I learned that 
my husband had sent the letter to your 
manuscript editor by mistake, and that it 
had been accepted for publication as 
“HONEYMOON IN TEHERAN!”
