Ever since I was a little boy, I always thought

It was a carnival and things weren't 
^working out" — whatever that meant

asked if I had experience with bears. Did 
I?!? Was there time to tell him about all 
the pictures of bears I had cut from 
magazines? All the Bears and Cubs
