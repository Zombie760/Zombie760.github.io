1 am responding to your question 
“Could there be a curse of MAD?” 
| would like to state for the record that 
there most definitely IS a curse of MAD. 
I have been reading MAD since it was a 
lowly EC comic book and I was a bright 
but naive youngster. The curse of MAD 
followed me throughout my teen and 
college years and on into adulthood as 
a professional journalist. It seemed | 
could not escape its thrall. It was always 
there, lurking in the background, 
through the turbulence of the 1960s, the 
crisis of Watergate, the agony of 
Vietnam, the insipid Reagan years, the 
deceit of two Bushes, continuing con- 
flicts in the Middle East, scandals and 
outrages, wars and rumors of wars and 
general malfeasance in the corporate 
world and even sports. It was the curse 
of MAD that taught me to look behind 
the headlines and the corporate and 
political spin and crapola. It forced me 
to think for myself and to look for the 
irony and truth in life. The curse of 
MAD taught me to always question 
authority and never take life too seri- 
ously. Yes, the curse of MAD does 
indeed exist and | have been blessed 
with it all my life.
