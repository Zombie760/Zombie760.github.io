I noticed an error on page 18 of MAD #490. You show Steven Spielberg 
at his desk, which has two Oscars on it. This was probably done because 
Spielberg has won two Oscars for directing. The reason this is an error 
is because Spielberg also won two Oscars for producing: he won a 
Thalberg Award at the Oscars in 1987 for his career achievements as 
producer and he earned the 1993 Best Picture Oscar for Schindler's List. 
So you see, four Oscars should have been drawn on Spielberg's desk, 
not two. Next time you attempt to draw someone at their desk, I suggest 
you do more research to see how many Oscars they've won!
