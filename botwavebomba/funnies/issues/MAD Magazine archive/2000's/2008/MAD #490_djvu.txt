I cannot imagine what possessed you guys to pass off 
an entire issue of MAD as “written and drawn by monkeys;" 
but whosever idea this was, I'm sorry to say it wasn't the 
least bit funny. The lengths you all had to go to try and 
milk monkey-related gag lines out of every panel in the 
magazine, were all too painfully apparent, even to the 
point of using the same “jokes” over and over again. OK, 
monkeys love to fling poo, we get it, move on. I have a 
friend who's a big fan of Monk and I didn't even bother 
showing him your supposed satire because it's more 
chimp humor than actually poking fun at the foibles of 
the character. Certainly, you can come up with a snarky 
reply to my letter, and even make it look as if the reply 
was written by a monkey, but the bottom line is still the 
same — in all my 40 or 50 years of reading MAD, this is 
the first issue I can recall that was totally without a shred 
of humor from cover to fold-in. 


Steve Haller, Oak Park, MI 


Hallerback Boy — We're so sorry you didn't enjoy the Monkey 
issue. There's nothing we value more than the opinions of 
long-time readers. So we stayed up all night and came up 
with an exciting idea that we think you'll really get behind. 
Look for an upcoming special issue of MAD written and 
drawn entirely by cranky, whiny, boring old men! (Snarky 
enough?) Thanks for writing! —Ed.

traditional Craft Services Table to feed the cast and crew with an Early Bird Special, Keep it under your hat, but Harrison's 
people are insisting on soft foods only.

- Benign, vaguely-threatening Raiders/Last Crusade Nazis = Good 


- Sadistic, Holocaust-causing Schindler's List Nazis = Bad

Christensen. That way I can be sure his performance doesn't distract the audience from all the cool special effects.

I got your note about making sure this movie is historically accurate, and I agree. Since Crystal Skull takes place in 1957,

attack. But, you know, in that fun, lighthearted Indiana Jones way.

flicks, and I’m running out of ideas. Hey, here’s a thought: what if we had him sit next to then-
