IMPORTANT PRODUCT RECALL INFORMATION 
Product: The Electronic Talking 
Alfred E, Neuman Doll 
Inthe wake of Dell Computers’ large-scale recall 
of laptops due to potentially-explosive batteries, 
we at MAD have been ordered to issue a recall 
of our Electronic Talking Alfred E. Neuman 

Doll. 


Because The Electronic Talking Alfred Е, 
Neuman Doll does not use batteries, there is 
Virtually no danger of a similar overheating 
situation. However, since the Alfred doll plugs 
directly into a L10-volt wall outlet, there have 
been several reported instances of a lethal shock 
being sustained by consumers who misuse the 
product — such as by attempting to make it talk. 
Furthermore, it has been determined that 
lead-based paint was used for Alfred's freckles, 
eye-brows and other facial features. Large 
amounts of mercury and other toxic chemicals 
were used for Alfred's hair coloring and 
clothing. Lastly, the tiny ball bearings used to 
weigh Alfred's feet may spill out of the seams of 
the doll and present a choking hazard if they are 
swallowed. (If not swallowed, they still consti- 
tute a severe health hazard as they are made of 
compressed arsenic. So it's bad news all around.) 
If you find any of the above severe health 
hazards troubling, you can return your 
Electronic Talking Alfred E. Neuman Doll 
to us. Prior to shipping, please consult 
Federal Publication No. 23423ARG43 — The 
Interstate Shipping of Illegal Toxic Chemicals, 
Bio-Hazards Waste Products & Chemicals of 
Mass Destruction guidelines, 
We at MAD have always had the utmost respect 
for this country’s strict product safety codes, 
Although we rarely adhere to those codes, we 
certainly respect them.
