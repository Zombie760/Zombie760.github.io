Just thought I'd clear up a slight anatomical error 
in your article “Crimes of Fashion" (MAD #338). In 
that article, you made reference to "the dark recess- 
es of the gluteus maximus (crack)." The "crack" 
between the two buttocks is actually referred to as 
the natal cleft. The gluteus maximus is the muscle 
in each buttock. Near it are the gluteus medius and 
the gluteus minimus. These three muscles are 
known as the glutei. They serve to rotate and 
extend the thigh.
