A BLOATING VICTIM 


Garfield the cat, 19, died yesterday of excessive bloating. 

“He couldn't control his eating,” said his owner Jon Arbuckle. "In 
the past few months his weight ballooned to over 80 pounds. He'd 
waddle a couple of steps or so, then collapse from the weight. 

An autopsy performed today revealed that Garfield's stomach 
contained a partly digested pizza, half a chocolate donut, the 
remains of a carp, three Twinkies and several body parts identified 
as once belonging to Odie the dog. 

Following the funeral, Arbuckle intends to reassemble Garfield's 
corpse and permanently attach it to the rear window of his car.
