only longer wearing, but because some models have a great 
deal of stretchability, you can put both feet into one sock, 
thus making a pair last even longer. 


CR's test

one week in a solution of milk and butter to determine their 
stain resistance. 

Then, as a ‘mud’ substitute, a mixture of flour and eggs

high temperature summer wear, the socks were then placed 
in an oven pre-heated to 400 degrees and left for 30 
minutes,
