conflict. In South Vietnam there are

But our immediate concern is that

we have a firm commitment, have 
invested lives, treasure and prestige, 
and have Americans to protect as we 
progressively withdraw.

control of Laos and Cambodia, a large 
portion of the more than 140,000 
Communist troops now engaged in these

South Vietnam. To date, Hanoi has 
rejected diplomacy and spread the 
conflict. 


The Laos Government for many years, 
and the Cambodian Government this year,

assistance...

disappointment.
