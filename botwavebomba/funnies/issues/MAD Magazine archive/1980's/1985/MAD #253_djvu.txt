HOT OFF THE PRESS DEPT. 


Two very confusing reports have appeared in our daily newspapers lately! The confusing 
thing about them is that sometimes they appeared right next to each other...like this: 


TEST SCORES SHOW 
KIDS GAN’T READ POPULAR WITH TEENS 


Taken together, these two items indicate that young people spend millions each year on 
books they can’t read. Which doesn’t make any sense. What does make sense is that most 
kids really can read...but they’re so bored with the dull writing in stuffy textbooks 
that they’ve turned to trashy novels. So if educators ever hope to lure students back
