1. It shall henceforth be a misdemeanor for medical office 
personnel to require any suffering person to fill out a “new 
patient” form in the doctor’s waiting room before receiving emer-

patient is required to fill out a second form when blood dripping

unnecessary data as (a) mother’s maiden name, (b) address
