The Method

Robert Caro had a researcher. So did Stoller. So did every writer whose books name primary sources. The labor splits: the human picks the document, names the through-line, defends the voice. The team does the indexing, the fact-checking, the prose execution.

My team is software. RECORD verifies citations against six archives — Federal Register, court records, Vatican Apostolic Archive, SEC EDGAR, GovInfo, BnF Gallica — and refuses to ship a sentence that can't anchor. The Humanizer Pack enforces voice DNA (40% Ellis / 30% Palahniuk / 30% Thompson for the non-fiction; Child / Winslow / Ellroy for thriller). The Prime Directive gate halts the pipeline if a claim is unanchored.

Anyone with $20 and Ollama can run the stack. What most people don't have is the discipline to write the gate that says no, cut this sentence, you can't anchor it. That's the editorial work. That's mine.

We all compound knowledge. We all feed the substrate. Only those who unplug long enough to read the primary document tap the frequency that does not lie.
