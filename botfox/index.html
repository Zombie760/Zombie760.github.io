What's Inside

Five tools. One window.

BotFox isn't just a browser. It's the toolchain you'd otherwise pay five subscriptions for.

🛡️

Anti-Detect Browser

Camoufox engine (Firefox-based, 49+ C++ patches). Multi-window pool. Device fingerprint spoofing. Proxy injection per window. No Chromium fingerprint — real Firefox.

Purge — Data Broker Opt-Out

Automated form-fill for 8 brokers (Spokeo, TruePeopleSearch, BeenVerified, more). Manual evidence for 20+. No competitor has this. Opt out of the internet from your browser.

WaveSox Proxy Pool

6,721 validated SOCKS5 proxies. Live refresh. Latency testing. Country/ISP filtering. No per-GB charges. Your pool, your rules.

Fingerprint Detection Suite

8-site test suite: creepjs, browserleaks (canvas/webrtc/fonts), fingerprintjs, pixelscan, intoli, amiunique. Know if you pass before you deploy.

Profile Manager

Persistent browser profiles with device configs, proxy bindings, and use tracking. Clone, delete, export. Your profiles stay on your disk.

🔌

REST API + WebSocket

20+ endpoints. Start/stop/navigate per window. Proxy list/test/refresh. Purge search/opt-out. Detection full/express. WebSocket heartbeat. Automate everything.
