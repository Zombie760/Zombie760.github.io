A primary-source journalism system built on the TELOS+PAI substrate. Monitors 221 news sources across civilizational lines, pulls primary documents, verifies every claim against a filed source before anything gets written.

## Recent outputs

### California Medi-Cal / Childcare Fraud: Federal Paper Trail

The OIG documented 180 indictments, 221 convictions, and $544 million recovered from California Medicaid fraud between 2021 and 2023. Minnesota's Feeding Our Future case — 78 federal defendants, mastermind convicted, $250 million — is the direct precedent for what's being filmed in California today. The ghost facility mechanism is identical. The indictment language maps straight across.

## Method

Every investigation starts with the 221-source news cache — Western, adversarial, and non-aligned outlets, translated before claim extraction. The Prime Directive gate then checks every claim against a named, filed, publicly verifiable primary source. What passes the gate gets written. What doesn't gets cut.

This is the same pipeline that produced *Unwarranted Influence* (1,100+ citations, every claim anchored to a filed document) and *Did Not Step Forward* (the Captain and the Champ, 1960–2016).
