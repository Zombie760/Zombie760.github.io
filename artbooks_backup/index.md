## STATIKVOID OMNIBUS

**The Complete Visual Archive**

The omnibus combines two distinct visual sequences into a single volume:

- **Part I: The Cartoon** — 100 frames from the Five Overlords saga
- **Part II: Lovecraftian Visions** — 45 images (El Vacío, Seraphina, Orion, Malcom, Viktor)

**Total:** 145 images with scene titles and captions

**Voice DNA:** 30% Metalocalypse / 25% Palahniuk / 20% Ellis / 15% Thompson / 10% Lovecraft

### The Five Overlords

| Overlord | Territory | Domain |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| El Vacío | Americas | Algorithm, Perception |
| Orion Titanium | Europe/Africa | War, Reality TV |
| Malcom | Asia | Sports, Gambling, Fixing |
| Viktor | Russia/Post-Soviet | Plagues, Bio |
| Seraphina | Operations | The Blade, The Eraser |

### The Doctrine

- Control perception, not reality.
- Reality is expensive; perception is cheap.
- Never let facts interfere with the narrative.
